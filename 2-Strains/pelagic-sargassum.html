The checked paper (DOI 10.1016/j.fochx.2025.103307) studies (E,E)-2,4-decadienal formation in sugar-smoked chicken thighs. It does not establish the historical Pelagic Sargassum/Sargasso Sea source assignment. A shared decadienal name is insufficient to equate positional/geometric isomers or to transfer a food-chemistry occurrence to a marine source. Stored identity, structure, assays and sampling gates are unchanged; occurrence in Sargassum is not ruled out. The inspected 2003 paper studies two geometric isomers of 2,4-decadienal generated in a crude Ulva conglobata enzyme preparation after added arachidonic acid. The alga came from an intertidal site at Hikoshima, Yamaguchi. This does not authenticate the historical Pelagic Sargassum/Sargasso Sea source or choose one geometric isomer for the unspecified stored graph. It is not recorded as direct isolation from untreated Sargassum.
