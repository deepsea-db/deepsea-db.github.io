X-ray crystallographic structure determination and refinement

Crystals from12mg/mL human MetAP2 in10mM Hepes pH7.2,10% glycerol,100mM NaCl; reservoir15–30%t-butanol,33–100mM sodium citrate pH5.2–5.6,4°C. Add1μL50mM fumagillin in ethanol to crystallization drop and equilibrate12h; diffraction1.8Å. No enzyme IC50 inferred.
