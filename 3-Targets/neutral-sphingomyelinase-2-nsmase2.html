Review details

This paper directly tests commercial altenusin as an inhibitory control against recombinant human nSMase2. The result is qualitative; no IC50 is read from the bar chart. T28 rat-brain neutral-sphingomyelinase Ki20μM/IC5028μM is a different preparation and cannot be transferred as human nSMase2 potency. The historical stored label is retained; the tested object is explicitly stated to prevent assuming a different sphingomyelinase isoform. The old label Sphingomyelin phosphodiesterase2 is ambiguous with a different neutral-sphingomyelinase isoform; the label is corrected to the explicitly assayed nSMase2,without assigning a gene/accession.
