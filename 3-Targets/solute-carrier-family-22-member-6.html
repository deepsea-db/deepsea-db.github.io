Review details

The primary article reports competitive inhibition by commercial Citrinin of human hOAT1-mediated [3H]ochratoxin A uptake in transfected mouse S2 cells. This is a functional transport experiment, not direct binding. Full-length human transporter cDNA in pcDNA3.1 was used; the paper specifies hOAT1-1 for the OAT1 experiment. The reported commercial-sample Ki is retained in the evidence audit, separately from the inhibitor dose. Citrinin-specific structure, stereochemistry and sample-form documentation are absent, so correspondence to the stored stereochemical structure is not established and numerical target potency remains withheld. The commercial preparation does not establish marine origin or resolve the existing source restriction; no protein accession or sequence identifier is assigned.
