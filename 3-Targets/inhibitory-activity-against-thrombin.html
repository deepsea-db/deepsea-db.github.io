Experimental co-crystal structure at 1.7 Å resolution; electron density supports a Ser195 tetrahedral hemiketal. No IC50, Ki or KD is assigned from this structural experiment.

X-ray crystallographic structure refinement

A 10-fold molar excess of CtA was added to the human alpha-thrombin–hirugen complex. Seed crystals used 30% PEG4000, 0.2 M sodium acetate and 0.1 M Tris pH8.5; repetitive sitting-drop seeding yielded the diffraction crystal. No thrombin crystallization temperature, protein concentration or incubation duration is assigned.

This is structural evidence, not a docking prediction or a newly measured potency.
