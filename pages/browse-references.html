Catalog, metadata & link rules

Inclusion in this catalog does not independently verify every compound association. Known years, titles and bibliographic fields come only from recorded citations; missing fields are not inferred. Identical external identifiers are displayed once per entry. Sources and assay records link only when an identifier agrees within the same compound and no other provided identifier contradicts it. Similar titles, matching organism names or evidence from another compound do not establish a link. Recorded roles and restrictions remain attached to each citation.
