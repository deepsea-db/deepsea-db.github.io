Publication scope and release

Three connected evidence dimensions

Compound records connect chemical identity and properties, biological sources and sampling context, and experimental activities with target annotations. Inclusion, protein identity and direct binding are separate evidence decisions.

The operational deep-sea subset uses current qualified source depths of at least 200 m. It is conservative: cross-bin or mixed-depth records can remain Unknown. Accepted marine or RCDB scope does not by itself establish deep-sea provenance.

Display bins: Shallow <200 m; Slope 200–<1000 m; Deep 1000–<6000 m; Hadal ≥6000 m. Verified sea shoal and coastal subtidal seagrass habitats also qualify for Shallow. A primary source that explicitly identifies the sample or strain as hadal qualifies for Hadal. Qualitative observations carry no numeric depth value. Unknown does not mean all primary papers lack depth.

Access and maintenance

This is a locally validated release candidate. A permanent HTTPS URL has not yet been assigned or tested. Public hosting and database upload require a separate authorized deployment. Structure search and arbitrary compound comparisons require the Python/RDKit service; the standalone static site cannot provide those computations.

Keep raw evidence, restrictions and old releases. Rebuild through shared templates, validate the complete site and retain a rollback before replacement. External reference-library snapshots and primary PDFs are not included in the publication download.
