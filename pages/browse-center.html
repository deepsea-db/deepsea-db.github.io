Collection, evidence & scaffold scope

Membership follows the unchanged RCDB registry. Center-reported material holdings are recorded separately from literature isolation provenance. Admitted evidence rows follow the existing evidence projection; displayed activity records on a compound page may also contain qualified or restricted entries. Neither count represents independent experiments. Stored source profiles can include other reported origins of the same compound. Classification and structure restrictions remain attached to each record. Historical scaffold links are descriptive and require current eligible structures.
