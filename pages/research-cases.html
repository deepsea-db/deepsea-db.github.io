Research examples

Three routes through one evidence framework

Start with a producer, a compound or an experimental target. Each route joins chemical records to the particular biological source and measured result, then returns to the original publication. The examples show database use; they do not add new experiments or establish therapeutic efficacy.

CASE 1

From actinomycete diversity to cell activity

Which reported microbial sources connect to a compound with a traceable nanomolar cellular result?

Reported producer
Streptomyces caelestis AW9-9C
Sampling context
Red Sea coastal waters near Jeddah, Saudi Arabia; sampling depth not reported.
Scope and identification
Related marine extension. This case does not establish deep-sea provenance. The ambiguous printed coordinates remain withheld.
Selected products
Citreamicin ε A · Citreamicin ε B

    Open S. caelestis AW9-9C and inspect its Red Sea collection description, reported identification evidence and two associated products. Depth remains unknown.

    Open AW9-9C →

  3. 3. Resolve the compound and its own assay

    Follow citreamicins ε A and ε B to the HeLa experiments. Both values are from the same 48 h MTT study, with the original uncertainties retained.

    Open the HeLa assay →

  4. 4. Return to the primary publication

    Use Table 1 and Figure 3 on PDF p4 for the cellular results, p3 for the MTT procedure and p2 for microbial collection. The caspase/apoptosis findings are not a direct-binding assignment.

CASE 2

From a deep-sea sampling record to compound activity

Can a cellular result be traced back to an explicitly reported deep-sea producing strain?

Reported producer
Penicillium senticosum RCDB005
Sampling context
South China Sea sediment collected at 1,400 m; numerical coordinates not reported.
Scope and identification
Depth-qualified deep-sea source. Reported ITS accession PX069401 is an evidence-linked identifier, not a new authentication performed by the database.
Selected products
Penicacid M

  1. 1. Start with sampling and identification

    Open RCDB005 to inspect the 1,400 m sediment source and reported accession PX069401. The profile connects 16 compound records. Identification details not stored in the profile remain unassigned; the primary article provides the ITS context.

    Open RCDB005 →

  2. 2. Follow the reported product

    Select penicacid M, identified as compound 16 in the article, and inspect its chemical record and source-specific provenance.

    Open penicacid M →

  3. 3. Keep the assay conditions with the result

    The MOLM-13 result comes from a CCK-8 cellular assay after 72 h exposure, read at 450 nm after a further 2 h reagent incubation. The displayed nM value is an exact rescaling of the reported μM value.

    Open the MOLM-13 assay →

  4. 4. Check the original evidence chain

    Verify compound 16 and its activity in PDF p9 Table 4, the producing strain and 1,400 m sampling depth in p10 Section 3.2, and the assay in p13 Section 3.8.

CASE 3

From a target annotation to the actual experiment

What was measured behind a target label, and which source context belongs to the compound?

Reported producer
Marine-derived Streptomyces sp.; strain unspecified in the reviewed source article
Sampling context
Marine-derived culture reported in a separate 2021 article; locality, water depth and coordinates not specified.
Scope and identification
Related marine extension. The 2008 assay uses piericidin A1 as reference compound 5; it does not identify that test material as a product of the iromycin-producing Dra17 strain.
Selected products
Piericidin A1

  1. 1. Enter through the target annotation

    Open Mitochondrial complex I; NADH oxidoreductase and follow the primary functional row for piericidin A1.

    Open the target record →

  2. 2. Inspect the tested preparation

    The measured preparation is bovine-heart submitochondrial particles, with NADH oxidation as the functional readout. It is not an isolated single-subunit binding experiment.

    Open the functional assay →

  3. 3. Separate function, cytotoxicity and origin

    The same 2008 paper reports L929 cytotoxicity separately. The stored marine-source report belongs to a different publication; its sampling gaps remain visible.

    Open the source context →

  4. 4. Verify the original records

    Check PDF p4 Table 1, p5 for the reference-compound and L929 descriptions, and p7 Sections 6.2–6.3 for the mitochondrial assay. The separate 2021 source report is Section 5.1.
