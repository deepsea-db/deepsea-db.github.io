How this analysis works & privacy

What this analysis returns

  1. Identifier matches, ambiguity and conflicts.
  2. Unit-normalized results with bounds and uncertainty preserved.
  3. Condition matches and explicit reasons for incomplete or incompatible comparisons.
  4. Original reference links and reproducible downloadable reports.

Your data stays here

Files are read in memory by this browser. This module sends no input to a server, uses no analytics or tracking cookies, and does not save uploads to browser storage. Clear the session or close the tab to discard them.

Reference links open external publications only when you choose to follow them. Downloads contain your supplied data; keep them private as needed.

Methods, tutorial & technical files
METHOD & REPRODUCIBILITY

A conservative, inspectable analysis

Exact identifiers are resolved without stereochemistry inference. Mass and molar units stay separate. IC50, EC50, GI50 and other endpoints remain distinct. Cross-study matches retain their study context. Source restrictions prevent quantitative use even when a number is present.
