The1991 primary article DOI10.1021/jo00013a044 isolates natural parents1-5 from Spongosorites ruetzleri, but its numbered methylated nortopsentin B derivatives6/7 are prepared methyl sulfate salts. In the primary figure both imidazole nitrogens are methylated and positively charged;6 retains one indole NH and7 methylates both indole nitrogens. The stored neutral N-Methyl nortopsentin B graph instead methylates both indole nitrogens and only one imidazole nitrogen, so it is not assigned to6 or7. The C22H19BrN4 EI fragment reported for7 is not proof of the stored neutral molecular identity. No parent sampling depth, source confirmation or P388/Candida result is transferred to this record.
