The inspected 2022 Cyclopiumolides paper reports compounds1/2 from Penicillium cyclopium SD-413. Its bibliography mentions neomaclafunginsA-I, not a numbered isolate of parent Maclafungin. This does not validate the stored Maclafungin/Actinoalloteichus SHA6 source; the separately recorded patent and original Maclafungin paper require their own review. Primary patent CN104877925A explicitly isolates Maclafungin B(3a),C(3b),D(3c), not the stored parent Maclafungin. The parent is a1998 background reference. Patent neutral formulas C44H76O11(B) and C45H78O11(C/D) differ from the stored C46H80O12. SHA6 is a reported marine strain, but family-level production does not establish this exact parent-source association. The historical citation wording is retained for audit and is not accepted as proof. No patent sampling, graph or activity is assigned to the parent.
