The primary paper reports dragmacidon A (8) from Hexadella specimens collected by SCUBA at approximately 40 m in Agamemnon Channel, Jervis Inlet, British Columbia. Its separate 100-200 m submersible specimens concern bromotopsentin (2), so that depth must not be transferred to this occurrence. The actual biosynthetic producer remains unresolved. Existing sampling-analysis restrictions and evidence states remain in effect.
